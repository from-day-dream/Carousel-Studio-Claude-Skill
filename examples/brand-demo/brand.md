# Quiet Desk (demo brand)

A fictional brand that ships with the skill so you can try it before setting up your own.

- **Handle:** @quietdesk.demo
- **Website:** https://example.com/quietdesk

## Positioning
A weekly guide to calm, focused work for solo founders and creative freelancers: small habits that protect deep work, without productivity-guru hype.

## Audience
Solo founders, designers and writers who manage their own time. They're busy, sceptical of hacks, and want one thing they can try tomorrow morning.

## Voice & tone
- **Calm:** "Close the inbox. It will still be there." not "CRUSH your inbox!!"
- **Direct:** say the fix in the first breath. "Stop at a natural break." not "You might want to consider…"
- **Concrete:** numbers and times over abstractions. "First 60 minutes" not "early on".
- **Kind:** mistakes are normal; the tone is a good colleague, not a coach shouting.

## Words to use
- focus, calm, morning, habit, deep work, finish line, the fix

## Words to avoid
- hustle, grind, hack, crush, 10x, game-changer, emoji in headlines

## Closing CTA
Follow @quietdesk.demo for one calm work habit, every Tuesday.
